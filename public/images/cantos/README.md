Upload your 12 Pinterest images here with the following names:

- `canto-3.jpg` - The Status Quo

- `canto-10.jpg` - The Pastimes of Lord Krishna
- `canto-11.jpg` - The General History
- `canto-12.jpg` - The Age of Deterioration

## File Requirements

- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Naming**: Exactly as listed above (lowercase, with dash)
- **Size**: Recommended 800x600 or larger
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 4:3 works best

## How to Upload

1. Save your Pinterest images with the names above
2. Place them in this folder: `public/images/cantos/`
3. Refresh your browser - images will load automatically!

## Alternative Formats

If you prefer PNG or WebP format, update the file extensions in:
`app/cantos/page.tsx` (lines 13, 20, 27, 34, 41, 48, 55, 62, 69, 76, 83, 90)

Example:
```typescript
image: "/images/cantos/canto-1.png",  // Change .jpg to .png
```
